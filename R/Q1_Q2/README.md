## data description - online\_cohort1 and online\_cohort2

This data was collected during the online training (phase 1) in 2021-2022 (Cohort 1) and 2022-2023 (Cohort 2).

## variable description - online\_cohort1 and online\_cohort2

* Schritt: training step this row is referring to
* Team: individual team ID
* Datum: date when the video to this particular training step was submitted to our online System
* Tage\_Ziel: absolute number of days from beginning of the training (Training step 1) to this date
* Differenz\_Durchschnitt: deviation from average duration to reach a training goal (goal achieved = yes) in phase 1 and 2 (online training), in days
* Ziel\_erreicht: was the Training target achieved? (1 = yes, 0 = no)
* Belohnung: reward type used, food, toy, or both
* Anzeigedauer: alert Duration, the total duration of the alert in seconds (could be longer than necessary for this step)
* Mindestdauer\_Anzeige\_erreicht: was the actual alert duration equal to or longer than the necessary alert duration for this training step?
* Anzeige: indication categorie based on signal recognition Theory (Gadbois \& Reeve 2016): true positive: the dog correctly indicated the sample odour; true negative: the dog did not indicate because there was no sample odour (empty search); false positive: the dog indicated although there was no sample odour; or false negative: the dog did not alert although the sample odour was present.
* Stoerreiz: number of distractions seen in the video, e.g., other dogs, traffic, wildlife; between 0 and 3
* Bemerkung: Comments, particularly to the distractions but also other observations in the video that could not be coded
* FCI\_Gruppe: FCI group according to Federation Cynologique Internationale (2025)
* Rasse: Breed of the dog according to Federation Cynologique Internationale (2025)
* Alter: age of the dog at the beginning of the training
* Erfahrung: team experience, 0 = none, 1 = dog sports, 2 = tracking and general scent work, 3 = scent matching or object search
* Geschlecht: sex of the dog
* Verstaerkerwahl: reward\_type, type of reward used for the dog (food, toy, both)
* Anzeigeverhalten: Chosen alert behaviour for initial training (freeze, sit, or lay and stare at target)
* Zielart: target species the dog was trained on: *F. spp.* complex, *A. artemisiifolia* or *I. glandulifera*
* Zielobjekt: sample odour, only relevant for cohort 1: whether a training step was performed on Kong or plant
