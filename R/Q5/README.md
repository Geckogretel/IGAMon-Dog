## data description - einsatztest

This data was collected by independent participants at their own schedule in 2022-2024, but also contains data from the online Training 2021-2022 as well as those collected at the practical seminars on 2-3 March and 8 May 2022 (Cohort 1)/ 22-23 April and 8 May 2023 (Cohort 2). Data from online Training were taken from the online training dataset, motivation scores were taken from an evaulation by the participants (S3 Table).

## variable description - einsatztest

* team: individual team ID
* FCI: FCI group according to Federation Cynologique Internationale (2025)
* Breed: Dog breed according to Federation Cynologique Internationale (2025)
* Dog\_age: age of the dog at the beginning of the training
* Team\_experience: team experience at the beginning of the training, 0 = none, 1 = dog sports, 2 = tracking and general scent work, 3 = scent matching or object search
* Dog\_sex: sex of the dog
* Reward\_type: type of reward used for the dog (food, toy, both)
* Alert\_behaviour: the behaviour the dog shows on the target (freeze, lay or sit and stare)
* Target\_species: target species the dog was trained on: *F. spp.* complex, *A. artemisiifolia* or *I. glandulifera*
* diff\_goal: mean of deviation from average duration to reach a training goal (goal achieved = yes) in phase 1 and 2 (online training), in days
* rel\_goal: relative goal achievement, a normalized metric that quantifies the proportion of training steps in which a specific training goal was successfully achieved, relative to the total number of training steps filmed per team during phase 1 and 2 of online training; 0 indicates the goal was never achieved across any filmed step, 1 indicates the goal was achieved in every filmed step, values between 0 and 1 reflect partial achievement relative to the total available training steps filmed
* nr\_steps: number of training steps
* year: year of the practical seminar, representing the cohorts
* start 1: first test started during the practical Seminar (1 = yes, 0 = no, NA = Team did not participate)
* finish 1: first test finished during the practical Seminar (1 = yes, 0 = no, NA = Team did not participate)
* start 2: second test started during the practical Seminar (2 = yes, 0 = no, NA = Team did not participate)
* finish 2: second test finished during the practical Seminar (1 = yes, 0 = no, NA = Team did not participate)
* start 3: third test started during the practical Seminar (3 = yes, 0 = no, NA = Team did not participate)
* finish 3: third test finished during the practical Seminar (1 = yes, 0 = no, NA = Team did not participate)
* sum: number of points gained during the three tests at the practical seminar (sum of the six previous columns)
* nr samples: number of samples hidden during the practical seminar
* found samples: number of samples detected during the practical seminar
* detection rate: proportion detected at practical seminar
* final points: team score at practical seminar, teams received points for starting and finishing test sessions, points were multiplied by the proportion detected in practical seminar, numerical between 0 and 9
* nr fresh samples: number of fresh samples hidden during the tests in the practical seminar
* fresh\_test: proportion of fresh samples hidden during the tests in the practical seminar
* field\_test: participation at field test fine scale (0 = not participated, 1 = participated but not passed, 2 = artficial search passed (no video for wild search), 3 = wild search passed)
* field\_participation: participation at field test (0 = not participated, 1 = participated)
* det\_rate\_field: proportion of samples detected at the artificial search during the field test
* Conservation: Motivation to participate according to this field evaluated by the handler according, Likert-scale between 1 and 6
* Responsibility: Motivation to participate according to this field evaluated by the handler according, Likert-scale between 1 and 6
* Science: Motivation to participate according to this field evaluated by the handler according, Likert-scale between 1 and 6
* Community: Motivation to participate according to this field evaluated by the handler according, Likert-scale between 1 and 6
* Qualification: Motivation to participate according to this field evaluated by the handler according, Likert-scale between 1 and 6
* Self.efficacy: Motivation to participate according to this field evaluated by the handler according, Likert-scale between 1 and 6
* Work.life.balance: Motivation to participate according to this field evaluated by the handler according, Likert-scale between 1 and 6
* Career: Motivation to participate according to this field evaluated by the handler according, Likert-scale between 1 and 6
* Dog.training: Motivation to participate according to this field evaluated by the handler according, Likert-scale between 1 and 6
* cohort: cohort this team belongs to (C1 = cohort 1, C2 = cohort 2)
